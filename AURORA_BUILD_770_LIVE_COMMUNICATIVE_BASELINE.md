# Aurora Build 770 — Live Communicative Baseline

Authors: Sunni (Sir) Morningstar and Cael Devo

## Outcome

Aurora now has a live communicative floor, not only a communication-learning
ledger.  The floor is the downward articulation of the proposition that has
already traversed her constraint genealogy and possibility architecture:

`receiver relation -> X/T/N/B/A semantic state -> recursive causal reconstruction -> matching WARP operation -> grounded answer or honest boundary -> final speech authority -> receiver consequence`

When a richer response is grounded and preserves the current relation, it
remains authoritative.  When it becomes a stale learned hint, malformed
fragment, unresolved request, prompt echo, or receiver-inverting correction,
the genealogy-bound baseline becomes the final delivered response.

No external language model, intent-classifier shortcut, or historical
assistant answer was added.

## Live defects repaired

- Communication operations were selected before recursive causal
  reconstruction and therefore did not match the proposition later evaluated
  at the receiver boundary.  Selection now occurs over the final semantic
  geometry.
- WARP operation birth referenced an undefined family record.  The router
  swallowed the resulting exception, leaving repeated communication pressure
  without a new operation.  Integration now captures the actual family and its
  lexical-free applicability shape.
- Operations applied only to an exact applicability-family hash.  They now
  support bounded nearest-shape use while never crossing the
  question/assertion agency boundary.
- Imperatives were flattened as assertions, `glass` was mistaken for a verb,
  and `table` was split as an adjective because of its spelling.  The
  relational parser now preserves directive force and the causal event.
- A richer composer could overwrite a safe boundary with an unrelated learned
  hint or broken sentence.  A final communicative-baseline authority now
  enforces relation preservation without displacing a genuinely grounded
  answer.
- Prior claims could answer a new, unrelated question through superficial
  overlap.  Prior-claim reuse is now bounded by current semantic relevance.
- Receiver language such as “that answer missed my question” was not recognized
  as correction.  The understanding contract now derives correction from the
  answer/failure/question relation rather than requiring a fixed phrase.
- Boundary confidence was incorrectly collapsed to factual-answer confidence.
  Baseline confidence now measures the supported articulation boundary, never
  the truth of the still-unresolved answer.

## Communicative abilities supplied

- Preserve the active subject, relation, object, complement, unknown role, and
  speaker direction.
- Answer from live self-state or live genealogy when those structures actually
  ground the content.
- State an explicit, fluent boundary when no grounded answer exists.
- Fulfil directive form without pretending the requested content is known.
- Acknowledge a receiver correction, own the prior failure, and avoid reversing
  who misunderstood whom.
- Carry the WARP trial component through final speech and post-turn observation,
  so receiver evidence can validate or dissolve the operation normally.
- Keep provisional operations provisional: the acceptance conversation caused
  no unearned promotion.
