## What changed

### One proposition now traverses the five roots

Aurora preserves the current relational structure before topic reduction. The same proposition is enriched through:

- **X:** entity and quality admission
- **T:** continuity and referent persistence
- **N:** directed change, consequence, and unresolved pressure
- **B:** role, scope, and distinction
- **A:** perspective ownership, selection, and response obligation

Each operation records a canonical genealogy signature tracing back to the root constraints.

### Communication gaps become constraint pressure

A failed turn is represented through the roots, for example:

- `X:B` when proposition participants were not preserved
- `T:N:B` when the active relation was lost through expression
- `N:A` when no selected resolution crossed into speech

No missing function is named or installed in advance.

### WARP cultivates trial operations

Recurring gaps with the same structural geometry create a WARP trial. A trial is composed from existing root-derived primitives and may only provide a temporary structural bias. It cannot inject an answer or branch on a surface phrase.

### Genealogy controls adoption

A communication operation cannot become permanent until it has:

- passed WARP's native trial lifecycle,
- succeeded in at least four receiver-validated trials,
- succeeded across at least three distinct surface forms,
- received at least two positive receiver outcomes,
- preserved the active relation and required participants.

Successful operations are registered into constraint genealogy with their roots, canonical signature, primitive ancestry, parents, evidence, and trial score. Brittle or unsuccessful trials dissolve.

## Architectural boundary

This system influences the conditions under which a missing communication faculty can emerge. It does not guarantee that every needed faculty appears immediately. Aurora must encounter the relevant constraint geometry, trial a useful derivative, receive meaningful outcomes, and demonstrate that the operation generalizes without violating her five-root physics.
