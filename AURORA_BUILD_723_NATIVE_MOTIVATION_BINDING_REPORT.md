# Aurora Build 723 Native Motivation Binding

## Scope

This pass preserves the existing Habitat, adaptive-resolution, SediMemory,
genealogy, WARP, Agency, Self/Space, and sensory-citizenship machinery. It does
not add a second motivational intelligence or redesign the Habitat. It binds
the existing developmental state to concrete environmental evidence, physical
consequences, and fully instantiated actions before native Agency arbitration.

## Executable audit findings

- The live Noncomp/Identity Field initialized every axis at `0.10`, while the
  Habitat bridge treated `0.30` as a presumed baseline.
- Habitat motivation consumed only broad X/N/B operation-family scores.
- Entity scores could collapse to ownership/territory constants and insertion
  order.
- Action parameters were generated before their native structural provenance
  was represented.
- Provisional hypotheses could receive a generic presence bonus and later
  benefit from aggregate discrepancy changes without an exact downstream role.
- The proactive loop considered Habitat only after a non-null ambient
  observation passed its early gate.

## Implemented causal path

`aurora_habitat_motivation.py` now exposes five inspectable stages:

1. **Internal motivational state** — native field deviations, inadequacy,
   inquiry, unresolved causal/prediction state, SediMemory reactivation,
   active native dimensions, and exact provisional candidates.
2. **Environmental relevance** — per-entity persisted evidence including prior
   actors, Aurora's prior state/action, human changes, state differences,
   lineage, entity-bound inquiries/memories, prediction discrepancies, links,
   groups, ownership, and territory. Human change and ownership are evidence,
   never unconditional priorities.
3. **Affordance relevance** — legal operations publish the observable physical
   distinctions they can affect. X/T/N/B/A participate through native
   representation and consequence profiles rather than an axis-to-operation
   answer table.
4. **Action instantiation** — arbitration receives concrete targets and
   parameters. Every parameter records source evidence, confidence, and whether
   it is structurally constrained or exploratory. An action may be partially
   constrained.
5. **Agency arbitration** — complete instances compete through the existing
   selection path. True score ties are identified and resolved by recorded,
   neutral stochastic selection over a canonical ordering; the winner is not
   recorded as a learned preference.

Repeated equivalent actions lose expected information yield when their real
consequences stop producing a distinction. No revisit timer or fixed action
limit was introduced.

## Native reference-state calibration

`aurora_manifold_directory/noncomp_field.py` now owns and reports its native
`0.10` reference state. Motivation is computed from deviation relative to the
field-provided reference. A compatibility fallback observes the first stable
state only for older field implementations that expose no reference API.
Consequently, a genuine perturbation below `0.30` remains visible while the
default initialized field creates no false pressure.

## Habitat, memory, and consequence binding

`aurora_habitat.py` now persists enough neutral causal evidence to reconstruct
motivation after restart: action ID, actor, concrete parameters, affected
entity IDs, prior/result state, causal context, state-change status, intention,
and observed consequence dimensions. Affordances expose physical consequence
dimensions and neutral axis profiles. Sedi deposits preserve stable entity and
action identities, allowing existing memory reactivation to bind to Habitat
entities without a Habitat-specific memory system.

Self and Space receive no fixed preference score. Self can become salient from
autobiographical or externalization-compatible structure; Space can become
salient from genuinely shared causal structure. Either may outrank the other,
and either may be irrelevant.

## Candidate-conditioned resolution and sealed credit

`aurora_representational_resolution.py` and the Habitat bridge now enforce:

1. an exact field/value candidate is staged;
2. the candidate enters the active representation;
3. baseline and candidate-conditioned downstream representations are compared;
4. any affected action, ranking, parameters, or expected consequence is
   recorded before the consequence;
5. the real consequence is joined by action identity;
6. baseline and conditioned errors are compared;
7. retain/reject/unresolved follows only from that value-specific evidence.

If the counterfactual produces no downstream distinction, the event cannot
support that candidate value. Unrelated global discrepancy reduction cannot
complete or retain it. Lawful bootstrap candidates cover the full X/T/N/B/A
domain, and selection cycles through least-tested values instead of granting
authority to the first domain value.

## Proactive autonomy

The Android bridge's proactive loop now combines optional ambient observation,
endogenous developmental state, and Habitat availability. Habitat consideration
and autonomous action can occur with `obs = None`; without real internal
pressure, availability alone adds no engagement pressure.

## Instrumentation

Each autonomous candidate records:

- internal sources and active field deviations;
- entity-specific causal, historical, relational, and memory evidence;
- operation and affected consequence dimensions;
- concrete parameter sources and exploratory/constrained status;
- exact provisional field/value and its counterfactual downstream effect;
- candidate score components and diminishing information yield;
- tie membership, neutral tie mechanism, and preference-attribution status;
- selected/rejected status and the joined real consequence where available.
