# Aurora: uniform-crystal resolution, recall, reasoning and communication integration

**Constraint from Sunni:** All crystals are the same unified crystal architecture. This change **does not introduce a crystal type, specialized cognitive crystal, new crystal store, dedicated resolution crystals, or separate persistent memory**.

## Existing ownership retained

- A concept's established crystal holds its qualified `fk:<kind>` facets. They now carry up to six compact original occasions per entry, with event reference, basis, source, original context, consequence, polarity and ancestry. Original event journals remain authoritative.
- A learned constraint-axis path remains in its **existing** `lsa:res:<subject>:<path>` facet at the native coordinate. Its earned lexical associations are recorded in that very same facet as `word_evidence` and continue to populate the pre-existing `word` facets for ordinary composition.
- Hypothetical discovery remains in `hyp:*`, never upgraded by replay.
- No new `Crystal` class, registry, schema type, or alternate store is created by the integration. Normal native crystals can still grow or mature as their original architecture allows.

## Actual source → recipient path

1. Experience is observed once by the existing resolution ledger / representation exchange.
2. A representation that **earns** its status or loses earned standing is synchronized to the native `lsa:res` facet, rather than leaving a stale earned claim. Other synchronization remains on existing periodic/save cadence.
3. The ordinary crystal's `fk:condition`, `fk:episode_ref`, `fk:cause` etc. keep qualified contextual occasion references. A correction may decrease the current projection's strength; original evidence remains part of the historical record.
4. A bounded read in `aurora_live_experience.crystal_context_for_turn` retrieves only the relevant existing facet contents and their earned word associations. One transient turn-local cache prevents duplicate retrieval during the same turn. No recall creates a new evidence event.
5. The existing thought braid's **memory process** gets crystal relevance and axis orientation, in addition to SediMemory's existing strata; no second process kind is introduced.
6. Existing WorkingMemory contextual recall may consult the same qualified `episode_ref` facets, with explicit witnessed/told provenance required before offering a remembered quote.
7. Existing Confluence gathers the same crystal contextual evidence alongside web-asserted and already-derived knowledge, and annotates its grounded statements with the source facets. A bare axis path or word association is **not** turned into a factual sentence.

## Key safeguards

- No new factual assertion from co-occurrence alone.
- No learned status from dreamed, simulated, recalled or absent historical evidence.
- Historical interpretation and later evidence remain distinct in their own referenced occasions.
- Diminishing evidence is allowed to weaken current confidence; a stale maximum cannot conceal a correction.
- The same physical crystal object is shared by all functions. Facets distinguish the **content/evidence**, never the kind of crystal.
- Normal first-party cognition/grammar still determines what Aurora says. A memory or learned representation does not prewrite responses.
- No recomputation of the original history just to recall a previously learned path.
- Direct physical phone/desktop latency, behavioral quality, and inter-device continuity still require device tests.

## Limits to verify, not promises

This change makes existing qualified structures **reachable** during live reasoning, recall and communication. It does not prove that Aurora can now autonomously express every nonlinear path in ordinary language, that all semantic claims are grounded by those paths, or that every multi-source situation yields a better prediction. Those require observed turn-level consequence tests. The earlier six-turn probe never activated the structural perspective builder; this integration targets a different, already demonstrated learned-representation pathway.

Review regression results before merging. No other branch should reimplement a distinct crystal class or duplicate the source of truth.
