# Aurora Live Possibility Actualization — 2026-09-10

## Purpose

A live occurrence may support more than one computationally coherent continuation without allowing every considered continuation to become lived history.

The implemented boundary is:

`one frozen braid/self/King snapshot -> independent continuations -> Pareto evidence -> bounded inception projection when needed -> King identity-topology arbitration when still unresolved -> one continuity commit`

## Existing machinery reused

- `ThoughtBraid` supplies the non-consuming present-time cross-section.
- `ActiveSelfState` supplies the turn-local self model already used by thought integration.
- Subsurface `PredictiveStager` supplies divergent prospective frames.
- `ThoughtIntegrationSpace` resolves each prospective frame independently.
- Layer-7 `InceptionEntity` supplies an ephemeral inner-universe projection only when simpler evidence cannot distinguish surviving continuations.
- `NoncompField`, the King Quasicrystal identity field, supplies final identity authority from its own current-vs-reference X/T/N/B/A topology.
- `ThoughtContinuity.carry_forward()` remains the actuality boundary and runs exactly once, only for the selected continuation.

No persistent possibility self, second motivational intelligence, or global scheduler was added.

## King authority

The King does not override situational evidence. It is consulted only after ordinary evidence and deeper self-projection leave multiple non-dominated continuations.

Its arbitration is read-only and occurrence-frozen. The field is sampled once before sibling continuations are evaluated. Positive axis displacement above the King's own native reference state is normalized into a live identity topology, matching the identity-field deviation semantics already used by Habitat motivation.

Each surviving continuation contributes only the set of native axes it actually occupies through its ThoughtState fingerprint and predictive pressure perspective. No fixed personality weights are introduced. Identity congruence is cosine overlap between that candidate axis surface and the frozen King topology.

If exactly one continuation is more congruent, the King supplies the final identity discrimination and that continuation may cross the Agency actuality boundary. If the King has no active displacement, has no intersection with the surviving continuations, or finds them equivalent, no preference is fabricated. A final neutral choice may actualize one branch, but the trace explicitly records that the tie break was not evidence of preference.

## Isolation guarantees

- Rejected continuations never call `ThoughtContinuity.carry_forward()`.
- Rejected continuations never enter SediMemory.
- Candidate integration spaces receive copied turn contexts.
- The current braid frame remains behaviorally unchanged when it is the only viable continuation.
- Layer-7 hypothetical projection restores Python's global RNG state, so rejected futures do not alter later lived stochastic execution.
- The King identity field is read only. Arbitration never calls its ingest, reset, decay, or other mutation surfaces.
- Candidate order cannot change the neutral-equivalence result.

## DCF behavior

Deep simulation is conditional. If Pareto evidence leaves one non-dominated continuation, Layer-7 simulation is skipped entirely. Identity arbitration is likewise skipped unless simulation still leaves genuine competition. This preserves the same causal semantics on constrained phone hardware while allowing a future ACM executor to widen independent branches without changing their information boundary.
