# Reading the Information Circulation Map — 2026-11-12

**Authors:** Sunni (Sir) Morningstar & Cael Devo
**Map:** `docs/AURORA_INFORMATION_CIRCULATION_MAP.md` (generated; regenerate with
`python scripts/map_turn_information_flow.py --out docs/AURORA_INFORMATION_CIRCULATION_MAP.md`)

The map is generated by watching four live turns. This note is the only
hand-written part: what the map shows, in the terms of the Unified Directive.
Every statement below is a count or a name taken from the map.

## Why this exists

Faults were being found one at a time by zooming in, and repaired without the
whole in view. The directive's second deliverable is the instrument for that:
a map of how information enters, circulates, and has consequences. **Rule from
here on: a change is placed on the map before it is written** — what flows into
the stage it touches, what flows out, who reads it, and which standing
decisions are cited there.

## What the map covers, and what it does not

Covered: the turn's pipeline state (162 signals, written by 23 stages) and the
`systems["_…"]` bus as it appears in source (314 keys).

Not covered yet: fields on the turn-understanding object, and each organ's own
private state (grammar lineage, genealogy, lexical grounding, SediMemory). A
path that runs inside an organ is not on this map. Four turns were run; a path
none of them took is not shown.

## Trace 1 — the subsurface group, followed to its intended consumer

Step 2 of the plan, done for the first group. Each line is read from her code
or her state file; none is inference about intent.

**The design, as her own code states it.** `aurora._compress_at_crest` is the
surface crest: every contribution is ranked by salience = pressure × (1 +
charge), and "the subsurface crest enters as high-salience deep material".
`_gather_subsurface_contributions` and `_is_speech_like` both state the rule
for how: coherent guidance may enter as content; intuition signals "bias the
field, never the surface text … it shapes salience, not words".

**What actually happens.**

1. *The content route yields nothing.* `_gather_subsurface_contributions` reads
   `projection["conscious"]["guidance"]` or `projection["guidance"]`. The
   projection the subsurface writes has neither key; its text is under
   `surface_guidance`. Nothing from the subsurface reaches the crest as content.
2. *The salience route does not exist.* `_contribution_charge` has four
   sources of charge — relevance to the input, her emotional state, a question
   mark, live sensory presence. None is the subsurface. The intuition signals
   are copied into the turn state under three names
   (`subsurface_intuition`, `subsurface_intuition_signals`,
   `subsurface_intuition_signals_live`) and nothing reads any of them.
3. *What the projection carries is mostly prewritten.* In
   `dual_strata/subsurface_projection.py` the intuition signals are labels
   (`clarify`, `hold`, `steady`, `slow_down`) with English summaries, selected
   by thresholds; `surface_guidance` is a sentence chosen from fixed maps
   ("Surface should feel careful around interpretation while subsurface
   sharpens the boundary problem."). The parts that are her actual state are
   numeric: the weights, `readiness_bias`, `dominant_axis_hint`,
   `prediction_bias`.
4. *The projection on file is six months old.* `aurora_state/subsurface_projection.json`
   was generated 2026-05-17. The surface loads it from disk whenever no live
   one exists, and nothing checks its age.

**What follows for any repair.** Connecting route 1 as it stands would put
prewritten sentences into her crest — the kind of source her voice gate blocks,
and the kind of thing that may not enter her (hardening rule 8). Connecting
route 2 with the labels would do the same in miniature. The faithful repair is
for the subsurface's *own* state to reach the crest as pressure, which is what
"shapes salience" names. Her code does not yet say which subsurface quantities
those are; the candidate is the subsurface crest that
`build_subsurface_projection` accepts as `subsurface_state_dict["subsurface_crest"]`.
Finding that out means extending this map into the subsurface daemon. **No code
was changed for this trace.**

**Against the directive.** Part III says the subsurface contributes
"accumulated understanding, predictive framing, memory, and deeper cognitive
development" to the surface. As built, what it sends is a status report in
prewritten English plus a few numbers, and the surface uses none of it.

## Trace 1b — the conscious frame arrives by another route

The caution on Finding 1 turned out to matter, so it is recorded here rather
than left as a caveat. Of the 11 subsurface signals with no reader on the
pipeline state:

- **`subsurface_conscious_frame`, `subsurface_conscious_crest`, `present_frame`
  are redundant copies of information that does arrive.** The conscious frame
  is produced by the DCE bridge (`dual_strata/dce_bridge.py`: subsystem crests
  converge into a subsurface crest, then a conscious crest and frame) at the
  end of each turn and stored on the `systems` bus as `_live_conscious_frame` /
  `_live_conscious_crest`. From there it is read by the next turn's action
  selection (`aurora.py` ~20397), CERS hesitation (~19988, ~27241), the
  confluence (`aurora_confluence.py` 445), the activation field, the training
  pulse and the daemon. `_present_frame_snapshot` is read by the crest's charge.
- **The eight projection-derived signals are true dead ends** as traced above:
  `subsurface_effects`, `subsurface_intuition`, `subsurface_intuition_signals`,
  `subsurface_active_effects`, `subsurface_dominant_axis_hint`,
  `subsurface_readiness_bias`, `pre_generation_subsurface_state`,
  `dual_strata_runtime` (the last two not individually followed).

So her conscious-sense carrier (the DCE frame) is connected and consumed. What
is disconnected is the *projection*: the file-borne report from the subsurface
daemon. That is also exactly what the strata link's downlink carries.

**A limit of the map this exposed.** It watches the pipeline state live but
only indexes the `systems` bus from source, so it cannot show on its own that
one signal is a copy of another. Each "no reader" group still needs this kind
of follow-through.

## Placement — "hearing is not producing" (FIX-A123, PR #244)

Placed on the map by hand, because the path runs inside organs.

- **In:** heard text reaches `ExpressionPerceptionEngine.ingest_interaction`
  from four call sites per turn — the gateway's first synthesis (the user's
  words), the gateway's recall-enriched re-synthesis (the same words plus
  `[recalled:…]` fragments), her articulation drafts, and the post-reply
  persistent ingest.
- **Through:** `SentenceComposer.absorb` → `GrammarEngine.observe_exchange`
  → motif lineage (`record_success`, context hash) and, before the fix,
  `_log_relief_to_genealogy`.
- **Out, and who reads it:** the motif lineage feeds motif selection and the
  promotion gate (≥ 3 contexts). The relief log is a genealogy observation
  crediting `A:OUTLET_PUSH`, `B:INTERFACE_WEAKEN`, `T:ADVANCE_TICK`; the
  genealogy learns ability relief from its observations, which feeds which
  abilities are active in later turns.
- **What the relief observation is.** Its "before" pressure is computed from
  the clarity argument and its "after" pressure is all zeros, with fixed state
  signatures. On the absorb path clarity is the constant 0.6. So every heard
  sentence wrote the same synthetic full-relief observation.
- **Standing decisions at this site.** FIX-A016: heard language feeds the
  motif lineage — kept. Comm-credit Phase G (`tests/test_comm_credit_phase_g_live_wiring.py`):
  immediate credit is replaced by delayed receiver evidence through
  `record_motif_outcome` — the fix removes the one remaining immediate credit,
  which is what that decision already called for.
- **Verdict.** Consistent with both decisions; it removes a synthetic
  observation and a triple ingest, and adds no knowledge to her. One part is
  still open and deliberately untouched: her articulation drafts are absorbed
  as heard language.

## Trace 2 — the ability-effects group

**The four dead-end signals are breakdowns of a signal that is read.**
`active_ability_effects` (the expanded list) has four readers in `aurora.py`.
The raw list, the translation table, the passive list and the unhandled list
are detail views of it that nothing reads. That alone would be minor. What the
detail views *contain*, captured on live turns, is not.

**What her active abilities are on a live turn.** Per its docstring,
`_select_active_abilities` picks "which thinking operations are live this
exchange": up to five abilities whose axis is active, cheapest first. On
"I spent the morning fixing the fence behind the house." it selected:

    X:CODE_LINEAGE_477e4398cd, X:CODE_LINEAGE_334639ccf1, X:CODE_LINEAGE_55b832b7c2,
    X:CODE_LINEAGE_f8bd092c01, X:CODE_LINEAGE_3939e763ff

Their effect tags are lineage metadata (`complexity_axes:2`, `depth_score:0.0933`,
`generation:3`) and records of source edits (`manual_change:MC:…`,
`manual_source:aurora.boot`, `mutation_module:tests.test_heard_vs_produced_learning`,
`mutation_module:tests.test_strata_link`). The last two are test files written
during this very audit: at boot, changes to her source are registered as
abilities, and they were then chosen as her live thinking operations for a
sentence about a fence. The translation table for those effects was empty on
every turn captured.

**The proportions, counted on a booted instance.**

| Abilities in her genealogy | 13,352 |
|---|---|
| `CODE_EVOLVE` (records of code) | 12,250 |
| `CODE_LINEAGE` (records of code) | 583 |
| `LINK_L` | 356 |
| `COMPRESSION` | 126 |
| everything else (`X:ADMIT`, `T:DEFER`, `N:REUSE`, `B:SEPARATE`, sensory, …) | 37 |
| Abilities with at least one effect the turn can translate | **11** |

So 96% of what the genealogy holds as abilities are records of code, the
per-turn selection draws from that same pool with no notion of kind, and what
it selects carries nothing a turn can use. This is also the 13.7k-item set
behind the costs in the baseline (the ~5 s cold index build and the index
rebuilt on observation).

**Her design already speaks to this; it is not a new question.**
- Every function is meant to have a crystal and an XTNBA signature — so
  representing code is intended.
- Generalisation doctrine: operations that are the same concept with a slight
  variation in lineage are to be "categorized as one concept with
  differentiated variants rather than mapped one by one". 12,250 `CODE_EVOLVE`
  entries is the one-by-one mapping that doctrine rules out.
- Irreducibility doctrine: paths that divide her observations identically are
  one equivalence family, held as a single ancestry-sharing representation.
- Placement doctrine: in-depth function detail sits at the XTNBA quasi-crystal
  resolution; the surface carries a faded, low-resolution representation.

Read together: code records belong in her structure as families at depth, not
as individually selectable live thinking operations. **Nothing was changed.**
A repair here reshapes her genealogy and needs its own trace first: how code
abilities are registered at boot, what reads them at depth, and what the
selection would see once they are held as families.
